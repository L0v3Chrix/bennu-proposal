What a local AI agent actually does.

Not a chatbot. Not a widget. A local AI agent is a purpose-built program that runs on hardware we configure, install, and maintain — doing specific, repeatable digital work for your business every single day without your input.

Here's what your five agents do starting Week 1:

Agent 01 — The Guardian

Google Business + Reviews

Keeps all three GBP profiles active, accurate, and optimized. Monitors for new reviews across Google and Yelp.

Daily activity: Responds to positive reviews within 4 hours. Flags negative reviews to Chrix before they hurt your rating. Requests reviews from recent customers automatically. Adds posts, seasonal hours, photos on a rolling schedule.
Agent 02 — The Voice

Social Media — Instagram + Facebook

Creates and schedules content in Bennu's voice — the thrift store couches, the dusty books, the grumpy barista energy that made you.

Agent 03 — The Regular

Lapsed Customer Re-engagement

Your QR data captures every customer. Most haven't been back since the pandemic changed their habits. They didn't stop liking Bennu — they just stopped thinking about it.

Agent 04 — The Mapper

Local SEO + Search Visibility

When someone searches "24 hour coffee Austin" or "late night coffee near me" at 1:30 AM — you show up first. Not Starbucks. Bennu.

Daily activity: Monitors search ranking positions. Adjusts local SEO signals. Tracks how Bennu appears in AI search (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity). Keeps citations consistent across 40+ directories.

A direct line to your customer database. "Latte of the month." Event announcements. Flash deals. Simple, compliant, effective.

Daily activity: Sends weekly touch campaigns. Manages opt-ins and opt-outs. Tracks open and conversion rates. Reports monthly.

Program 02

The Blessing Bag Program

Instead of creating a place for them to come — create a reason to leave with dignity. A branded bag each location stocks for unhoused visitors.

  • Inside: snacks, hygiene items, a handwritten note from Steve & Stephanie, QR stickers
  • Optional participation: place stickers around Austin, photo-track placements
  • Tracks guerrilla marketing presence across the city
  • Recovery staff handles every interaction with grace
Month 2: Deployed at all 3 locations
Program 03

Recovery Community Sponsorships

Austin has 9–15 major recovery events every year. Bennu should be at every one. One peer recovery coach recommends you → their 5–15 clients start coming → they tell their sponsors.

  • Event sponsorships (table, flyers, verbal mention)
  • Recovery discount program (5–10% off — honor system)
  • Flyers at meeting locations across Austin
  • Direct outreach to ~650–900 peer recovery coaches
Month 2–3: First events sponsored
Program 04

Late-Night Street Team

70+ Austin bars close at 2 AM. Thousands of people suddenly need somewhere to go. Bennu is one of the only 24/7 indoor options — most of them don't know it exists.

  • Recovery community members earning honest income as canvassers
  • South Congress, 6th Street, Rainey Street — 12 AM to 2 AM
  • Trackable QR codes on every flyer
  • Real data on what neighborhoods convert
