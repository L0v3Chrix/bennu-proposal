The Model

What Torchy's Tacos Did —
Before They Were Torchy's

🌮 Case Study

Mike Rypka, Founder of Torchy's Tacos

Mike Rypka is in recovery. When he started Torchy's from a food truck in 2006, he faced the same challenge you're facing: hospitality workers who showed up late, called in constantly, and didn't care about the customer experience.

His solution: hire from the recovery community. People rebuilding their lives who show up early, stay late, and treat every shift like it matters — because it does.

He also offered recovery discounts, sponsored AA/NA events, and made Torchy's a known friend to the recovery community. The result? A fiercely loyal customer base that spread the word before Torchy's ever went viral.

Today: 110+ locations. No franchising. Started from a trailer in Austin.

This isn't a marketing gimmick. It's a staffing solution, a community positioning, and a growth engine — wrapped in something that actually helps people.

The Four Pillars

What we'll build together.

🤝

Recovery Hiring Pipeline

Staffing Solution

I'll connect you directly to Austin's recovery community through sober living networks, treatment centers, and peer support organizations. These aren't job boards — these are relationships I have. You'll have a steady stream of applicants who actually want to show up.

📦

The Blessing Bag Program

Homeless Solution → Brand Asset

Instead of asking your staff to remove unhoused individuals — which feels wrong and your employees aren't equipped to handle — we flip the script. A "blessing bag" with snacks, hygiene items, a kind note, and a stack of QR stickers. They leave with dignity. If they want to participate, they can place stickers around town and we'll track the results. Goodwill becomes guerrilla marketing.

🎪

Recovery Community Partnership

Event Sponsorship + Visibility

Austin has 9-15 major recovery events every year. Bennu becomes a sponsor. Tables at events. Flyers at meeting locations. A standing recovery discount. Peer recovery coaches know to send their clients to you. This builds slow, compounding loyalty that corporate coffee can never touch.

🌙

Street Team Coordination

Late Night Activation

Between midnight and 2 AM, people leave 70+ Austin bars looking for somewhere to go. We'll coordinate a street team — recovery community members earning honest money — to hand out flyers on South Congress, 6th Street, and Rainey. Trackable, measurable, human-powered marketing.
