Engagement Letter
Digital Footprint — 12-Month AI-Native Operations
Date: April 9, 2026
Valid through: April 23, 2026

Goal

Establish and autonomously manage Bennu Coffee's complete digital presence across all three Austin locations. Social media, Google Maps, reviews, SMS campaigns, SEO, and customer reactivation — running 24/7 through AI agent architecture hosted in a secure cloud environment. Your existing cashierless QR ordering system becomes the data backbone: every customer scan is a contact, a potential review, and a reactivation opportunity. You approve the voice once. We handle everything after that.

Our intent: This is an audition. We want to become your long-term technology partner — and we want you to stop looking elsewhere for a solution.

Scope of Work

  • Autonomous Social Media — Daily content across Instagram and Facebook that captures Bennu's voice — the weird Austin energy, the 24/7 vibe, the "come as you are" spirit. AI agents trained on your brand identity.
  • Google Maps Optimization — All three locations — MLK, South Congress, Highland — fully optimized with photos, hours, attributes, and keywords.
  • Review Generation Engine — Integrated with your cashierless QR ordering system. Every order scan captures a contact. Two hours later: an automated "How was your visit?" text with a one-tap Google review link.
  • Database Reactivation ("The Regular") — Your cashierless system has captured years of customer data. Targeted campaigns: "We miss you" nudges, birthday lattes, seasonal specials, and inactivity triggers after 60 days.
  • SMS Campaigns — Direct-line weekly texts: latte of the month, event announcements, flash deals. Compliant, easy opt-out.
  • Search Visibility (SEO) — Local SEO foundations so "24 hour coffee Austin" and "late night coffee shop" find Bennu.
  • Secure Cloud Environment — All agent architecture hosted in a secure cloud environment ensuring uptime and reliability. Monitoring, maintenance, and infrastructure included.

Out of Scope

  • Paid social media advertising / Google Ads
  • Custom website redesign or Shopify development
  • Physical signage, print media, or traditional advertising
  • Community Relaunch Program deliverables (see separate engagement)

Engagement Letter
Community Relaunch Program — 4-Month Engagement
Date: April 9, 2026
Valid through: April 23, 2026

Goal

Transform Bennu Coffee into Austin's recovery-friendly coffee shop through two equal pillars: a recovery community employment pipeline that solves your staffing challenges, and a QR sticker activation game powered by the Blessing Bag program that turns the unhoused situation from a liability into a brand asset. This is a hands-on consulting engagement — Genesis Labs designs the strategy, coordinates the network, and guides execution. Bennu handles the on-the-ground operations in a weekly working partnership.

Pillar 1: Recovery Employment Pipeline

  • Network Activation — Direct outreach to sober living houses, treatment centers, and peer recovery coaches. Candidates pre-vetted, motivated, and ready.
  • Candidate Pipeline — Ongoing sourcing, not a one-time list. The pipeline adapts as your staffing needs evolve.
  • Hiring Support — You hire like normal — we fix the pipeline. Interview coordination and warm introductions.
  • Peer Coach Network — Outreach to ~650–900 peer recovery coaches in Austin. Each coach influences 5–15 clients.

Pillar 2: QR Sticker Game + Blessing Bags

  • Blessing Bag Program — Bags with snacks, hygiene items, a note from Steve & Stephanie, and Bennu QR stickers. Distributed by recovery employees trained in compassionate de-escalation.
  • QR Sticker Activation — Recipients place Bennu QR stickers around Austin and optionally photo-track placements. Guerrilla marketing powered by goodwill.
  • Recovery Event Sponsorship — 9–15 Austin recovery events per year. Table presence, flyers, recovery discount program (5–10% off, honor system).
  • Street Team Coordination — Recovery community members canvassing South Congress, 6th Street, Rainey from 12 AM–2 AM. Flyers with QR codes, trackable results.
