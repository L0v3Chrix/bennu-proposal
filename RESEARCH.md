## 2. Coffee Shop Valuation (Industry Standards)

### Typical Multiples
- Coffee shops typically valued at **1.5-3x annual revenue** (lower end of restaurant multiples)
- Higher multiples for:
  - Strong brand recognition
  - Multiple locations
  - Loyal customer base
  - Unique positioning (24/7, community hub, origin story)

### Bennu Napkin Math (Conservative)
- 3 locations × $400-500K revenue each = **$1.2-1.5M/year**
- Down 20% = roughly **$240-300K lost revenue**
- At 2x multiple, that 20% = **$480-600K in valuation loss**

### If Consulting Recovers 10% Revenue (~$120-150K/year)
- Valuation impact at 2x: **$240-300K**
- This makes a $10-15K consulting engagement very cheap relative to upside
