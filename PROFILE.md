## Overview

- **Business:** Bennu Coffee — 24/7 coffee shops in Austin, TX
- **Founded:** 2009 (East Austin / MLK location)
- **Locations:** 3 (MLK/East Austin, South Congress, Highland/ACC area)
- **Problem:** ~20% revenue decline post-pandemic; Austin forgot about them
- **Notable history:** Founders created Chameleon Cold Brew recipe at Bennu → now national brand (Nestlé acquisition)

## Key Insights

1. **Late-night opportunity:** Austin's 70+ bars close at 2 AM — Bennu is one of the only 24/7 indoor options
2. **Recovery community:** 1M+ Texans in active recovery; untapped audience that needs non-bar third places
3. **Habit disruption:** Pandemic broke routines; lapsed customers never re-engaged
4. **The name:** "Bennu" = Egyptian phoenix (rebirth symbol) — fits the comeback narrative perfectly

## Meeting Notes — Chrix + Steve (2026-03-25)

**Approach:** Chrix didn't open the proposals — just listened and built rapport. Smart.

### Steve's Pain History
- Used internal staff → dissatisfied results
- Hired agencies multiple times → felt responsibility always got pushed back on him for things outside his expertise
- Could never justify spend or measure ROI against actual business movement

### What Steve Values (Core Standalone Services)
- Autonomous social media
- SEO / AI recommendation optimization
- Google My Business optimization
- Review automation
- **Self-admittedly has nothing in place** — old architecture nobody maintains

### 🔥 HUGE INSIGHT: Cashierless System at All 3 Locations
- Customer scans QR code → places order → pays via device
- Baristas just make drinks and clean
- **They're already capturing every customer's contact info**
- Opportunity: retention and ecosystem, not just acquisition

### Steve's Self-Awareness
- "Competing with a model that has almost been forgotten"
- Knows the brand has faded; open to fixing it

### Challenge #2: Staffing / Culture
- Current employee demographic is "less and less useful and helpful"
- Identified as a cultural issue
- **Chrix's solution:** Recovery community hiring pipeline
  - People in early recovery make excellent employees
  - Don't have the same challenges with difficult situations
  - Chrix can create path through local Austin recovery groups (online + offline) for applicants

### Challenge #3: Homeless Population
- All 3 locations frequented by homeless seeking shelter
- Steve & Stephanie are "good Christian people" — don't want to just kick people out
- Employees lack skills to handle situation gracefully
- Lose-lose: let them stay = lose customers, remove them = feels wrong

**Chrix's solution: The Blessing Bag Model**
- Instead of "a place to come" → create "a reason to leave"
- Care package / blessing bag given to homeless visitors
- **Inside the bag: stack of QR stickers** designed for them to place around town
- Optional participation: take photo of placement → send to tracking link
- Creates dignity, goodwill, AND trackable guerrilla marketing
- Recovery employees will handle these interactions better than current staff
- **Steve and Stephanie are excited about this**

### 🔥 Scalable Model
- If this works, it becomes teachable to other local business owners
- Chrix sees potential beyond Bennu — a replicable pattern

## Strategic Implications

The original proposal was heavy on **acquisition** (Night Owl, Fellowship, Street Team). But with cashierless data capture:

1. **The Regular becomes the highest-ROI agent** — they have the data, just not using it
2. **Review automation** — turn transactions into 5-star reviews automatically
3. **Loyalty/retention play** — low-hanging fruit before acquisition
4. **Acquisition agents still matter** but ecosystem/retention is faster win

**Basic AGE offer** should probably focus on:
- Social media automation (posting, engagement)
- GMB optimization + review automation
- The Regular (lapsed customer re-engagement using their existing data)

**Full Bennu build** adds the creative campaign layer (Night Owl, Fellowship, Street Team, Weird Austin, ground game).
